What we collect

When you book a service or visit the shop, we collect your name, phone number, email address, and your vehicle's type, make, model, year and mileage, along with the services we perform.

How we use it

We use this information to schedule and confirm appointments, keep a service history for your vehicle, prepare estimates, and, if you agree, send you maintenance reminders by text message or email.

Text messages and email

We only send reminders if you check the consent box when booking or tell us in person. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to a text or contacting us. Message and data rates may apply.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the shop's booking and messaging systems, and only as needed for that purpose.

How long we keep it

We keep your service history while you're a customer so we can track your vehicle's maintenance. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.
